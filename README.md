# Superstore Sales Analysis & Interactive Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyses the performance of a Superstore retail business using Microsoft Power BI. The goal is to transform sales data into interactive visual insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Evaluating overall sales and profitability.
- Identifying high-performing regions.
- Comparing product categories and sub-categories.
- Analysing customer segment performance.
- Identifying sales and profit trends over time.
- Highlighting business risks and opportunities.
- Providing actionable recommendations.

## Dashboard Features

The interactive Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total Sales KPI
- Average Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Month
- Sales by Year
- Profit by Month
- Year and Category slicers
- Category-level summary table

## Key Insights

1. The business generated approximately **2.30M in sales** and **286.40K in profit**, with a **12.5% profit margin**.
2. **Technology** recorded the highest sales and profit among the major product categories.
3. The **West region** recorded the highest sales and profit.
4. **2017** recorded the highest annual sales.
5. Monthly performance varied throughout the year, with strong performance toward the end of the year.

## Business Risks

- Uneven performance across regions.
- Dependence on strong-performing product categories.
- Opportunity to improve the overall profit margin.

## Business Opportunities

- Expand sales of high-performing Technology products.
- Improve performance in weaker regions.
- Capitalise on stronger year-end demand.

## Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing Technology products.
- Develop targeted strategies for weaker regions.
- Replicate successful strategies from the West region.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns for peak periods.
- Review pricing, discounts and operating costs to improve profitability.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI

- GitHub

### Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- Profit Growth

### Key Findings

- Sales Growth: **46.88%**
- Profit Growth: **48.42%**
- Consumer segment contributed **50.56%** of total sales.
- Central region recorded the lowest profit at **-39,706.36**.
- Furniture recorded the lowest category profit at **-18,451.27**.
- Several products generated significant losses, with the largest identified loss being **-8,879.97**.

### Business Problems Identified

1. Loss-making products
2. Regional underperformance in the Central region
3. Profitability concerns within the Furniture category

### Recommendations

- Review pricing, costs, discounts, and sales volume for loss-making products.
- Investigate the causes of negative profitability in the Central region.
- Reassess the performance of products within the Furniture category.
- Maintain strategies driving sales and profit growth while protecting profit margins.
- Continuously monitor KPIs through the Power BI dashboard.
